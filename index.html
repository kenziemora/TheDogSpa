Welcome to The Dog Spa

Every dog deserves some pampering! Here at The Dog Spa we offer a variety of high quality grooming services designed to make you and your pet feeling and looking the best. The goal of our spa is to provide a relaxing environment and provide top quality care.

Our Services

The Dog Spa offers a wide range of services including bathing, haircuts, nail trimmings, ear cleaning, brushing, as well as some fun cosmetic services. We leave it up to the client to decide what is best for their furry friend. Visit out services page to learn a little more about our services as well as pricing!

Did you Know?

Did you know dogs have different kinds of coats? Dogs can have single or double layer coats as well as having different textures such as curly, wire, silky, and hairless coats as well. Here at the Dog Spa we have experience with each one and know what kind of care is required to make your pet comfortable in their skin!
